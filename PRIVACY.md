# Verto — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: July 2026*

Verto is built so that this policy can be short.

**Verto does not collect, store, or transmit any data.** All file conversion and compression happens locally on your Mac. The app has no network capability, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no third-party SDKs.

The only information Verto keeps is on your own machine: your quality settings and a bookmark to the output folder you chose, stored in the app's local preferences so it can remember them between launches.

Your files never leave your computer.
